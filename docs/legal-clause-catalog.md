## Consumer Protection Act 1999 (Act 599)

Source: [KPDN official reprint](https://www.kpdn.gov.my/images/2024/awam/akta/ttpm/Act%20599.pdf),
as at 1 November 2021.

### ACT599-S9-B — Section 9(b)

> (b) in relation to services, is misleading or deceptive, or is likely to
> mislead or deceive, the public as to the nature, characteristics, suitability
> for a purpose, availability or quantity, of the services.

### ACT599-S12-3-C — Section 12(3)(c)

> (c) that the price covers or the method takes into account matters in respect
> of which an additional charge is in fact made;

### ACT599-S10-1-K — Section 10(1)(k)

> (k) concerns the existence, exclusion or effect of any condition, guarantee,
> right or remedy; or

## Consumer Protection (Electronic Trade Transaction) Regulations 2024

Source: [KPDN official PDF](https://repositori.kpdn.gov.my/bitstream/123456789/5299/1/PERATURAN%20URUSNIAGA%20PERDAGANGAN%20DALAM%20ELEKTRONIK%202024.pdf),
P.U. (A) 449/2024. The Regulations came into operation on 25 December 2024.
The extracted text below is an initial split; application to a particular
business remains subject to legal review.

### ETT2024-REG-2 — Regulation 2, “online marketplace”

> “online marketplace” means any electronic trade platform that is conducted
> through electronic means by any supplier;

### ETT2024-REG-3 — Regulation 3, disclosure of information

> (1) Any online marketplace supplier shall disclose the information specified
> in the Schedule on the online marketplace.
>
> (2) For the purposes of subregulation (1), any online marketplace supplier
> shall not disclose or provide any information as may be specified in the
> Schedule that he knows or has reason to believe is false or misleading.

### ETT2024-REG-4 — Regulation 4, language

> (1) The information disclosed by the online marketplace supplier on the
> online marketplace shall be in the national language.
>
> (2) In addition to the national language, any other languages may be used for
> the disclosure of information by the online marketplace supplier on the
> online marketplace.

### ETT2024-REG-6 — Regulation 6, error correction and acknowledgement

> (1) Any online marketplace supplier shall—
>
> (a) make available the appropriate means to enable the purchaser to rectify
> any error prior to or after the confirmation of the order made by the
> purchaser; and
>
> (b) acknowledge the receipt of the order to the buyer without undue delay.

### ETT2024-SCHEDULE-1-9 — Schedule, disclosure items 1–9

The Schedule lists the online marketplace supplier/company name; website
address, if any; email address and telephone number; address where the trade
or advertisement operates; main characteristics of the goods or services;
full price including transportation costs, taxes, and any other cost; payment
method; terms and conditions; and estimated time of delivery or service supply.
